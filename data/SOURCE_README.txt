This dataset contains Raman spectra of silicon recorded at room temperature in labspec6 (.ls6) and text (.txt) formats.
Raman spectra are raw (no processing were applied).

Date : November 25, 2021

Creator : Dr Thomas H. Kauffmann / LMOPS Spectroscopy Platform

Experimental conditions :
- Raman spectrometer : HORIBA HR Evolution
- Detector : CCD camera (1024 pixels, cooled at -70°C)
- Excitation wavelength : 532 nm
- Grating : 1800 gr/mm
- Microscope objective : 100x (Olympus)
- Confocal pinhole aperture : 100 µm 
- Spectral range : 50 - 4000 cm-1
- Time acquisition : 1 sec
- Accumulation : 1
- Polarization : no

Sample information :
- Sample of silicon wafer given by HORIBA used for Raman systems calibration.
